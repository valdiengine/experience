# Ecosystem Engagement & Gamification Rules Architecture

## 1. Strategic Vision

The visitor evolves from Tourist to Explorer, Observer, Contributor, Guardian, Ambassador.

Every action contributes to the knowledge and protection of the territory.

The ecosystem rewards exploration, ecological discovery, sports participation, community contribution, scientific observation, conservation actions, and local engagement.

The platform should not reward users for playing. It should reward users for knowing the territory, respecting nature, helping communities, promoting responsible tourism, and becoming guardians of destinations.

A person who visits a place leaves it better than before.

---

## 2. Engagement Domains

The ecosystem engagement model spans five domains:

Discovery - Places, species, experiences, destinations explored.
Ecology - Flora, fauna, marine observations, conservation actions, environmental reports.
Sports Exploration - Trekking, cycling, kayaking, surf, diving, running, outdoor activities.
Community - Memories, reviews, validation, events, local participation.
Education - Guides completed, ecological knowledge, challenges, learning achievements.

Each domain generates its own signals that feed into the dual economy system.

---

## 3. Dual Economy System

### 3.1 EcoTokens

Temporary utility points for immediate rewards, local benefits, experiences, discounts, and donations.

EcoTokens can be spent.

Earning examples:
- Species discovery: +5 EcoTokens
- Validated ecological report: +20 EcoTokens
- Community cleanup: +50 EcoTokens
- Completed destination mission: +100 EcoTokens

Spending examples:
- Local business discount
- Experience upgrade
- Conservation donation
- Badge customization

### 3.2 EcoScore

Permanent reputation score for user level, trust, ranking, badges, and access.

EcoScore cannot be spent.

EcoScore accumulates from all positive actions. It represents lifetime contribution to the ecosystem.

User profile displays:
- EcoTokens: 350 (spendable)
- EcoScore: 5420 (permanent)
- Level: Guardian

---

## 4. Eco Reputation Levels

Level 1 - Seed (0+): Can discover species, upload memories.
Level 2 - Sprout (100+): Can leave reviews, participate in basic missions.
Level 3 - Guardian (500+): Can validate community observations, join advanced missions.
Level 4 - Protector (2000+): Can create missions, lead community events.
Level 5 - Ambassador (10000+): Can create community initiatives, become destination ambassador.
Level 6 - Ecosystem Guardian (50000+): Recognized ecological contributor, mentor status.

Each level unlocks capabilities and responsibilities.

---

## 5. Eco Pokedex Expansion

The Pokedex is not only species collection. It is the Territory Living Pokedex.

Collections include:
- Species (flora, fauna, marine, fungi)
- Places (beaches, trails, viewpoints, heritage places)
- Experiences (kayak, hiking, diving, cycling routes)
- Businesses (eco accommodations, restaurants, local services)
- Destinations (regions, localities, communities)

The objective is to complete the knowledge map of each territory.

---

## 6. Sports Explorer System

Athletes become territorial explorers.

Supported activities: Hiking, Cycling, Trail Running, Kayaking, Surf, Diving, SUP, Climbing, Wildlife Photography.

Sports actions generate GPS routes, discoveries, observations, memories, and ecological data.

Example - Kayak: Route recorded, marine species observation, water condition report equals River Guardian Badge.
Example - Cycling: Route completed, places discovered, local businesses visited equals Coastal Explorer Badge.

---

## 7. Badge Engine

Permanent achievement badges in categories:

Discovery Badges: First Species Found, 50 Places Explorer, Destination Complete.
Ecology Badges: Ocean Guardian, Forest Protector, Biodiversity Observer.
Sports Explorer Badges: Kayak Guardian, Trail Explorer, Cycling Explorer, Deep Sea Observer.
Community Badges: Local Hero, Mentor, Community Leader.
Knowledge Badges: Bird Observer, Native Flora Expert, Marine Specialist.
Secret Destination Badges: First Explorer of Hidden Place, Seasonal Discovery, Rare Species Finder.

---

## 8. Mission System

Dynamic missions in types: Daily, Weekly, Destination, Ecological, Sports, Community.

Destination Mission example: "Discover Costa de Valdivia" requires visiting 5 places, discovering 3 species, uploading one memory, visiting one local business. Reward: +500 EcoScore and Costa Explorer badge.

---

## 9. Validation Rules

All ecological contributions require trust systems.

Validation levels:
Level 1 - AI Assistance: System suggests identification.
Level 2 - Community Validation: Other observers confirm.
Level 3 - Scientific Validation: Expert review required.

Rules: AI never replaces experts. Important species require validation. Protected species require scientific review. False reports reduce reputation.

---

## 10. Anti-Gaming Protection

Geographic validation requires GPS, timestamp, and location consistency.

Duplicate detection identifies duplicated images, repeated reports, and artificial farming.

Reputation protection: Bad behavior reduces EcoTrust, reduces rewards, and imposes temporary restrictions.

---

## 11. EcoTrust Reputation System

Quality reputation separate from points.

Metrics: Accuracy, Contribution Quality, Community Help, Scientific Reliability, Positive Impact.

Public profile shows: Name, Explorer Level, EcoScore, EcoTrust percentage, Badge count, Species discovered.

---

## 12. Local Destination Integration

Every locality has its own ecosystem progression.

Users can complete locality-specific Pokedex showing places discovered out of total, species discovered out of known, experiences completed out of available, businesses visited out of registered.

Completion generates locality-specific badges like "Guardian of Los Molinos".

---

## 13. Business Integration

Businesses participate without owning the ecosystem.

Businesses can receive Eco Seal, community reputation, visitor discoveries, and sustainable tourism ranking.

Examples: Eco Cabin receives "Protected Habitat Partner" seal. Restaurant receives "Local Ocean Friendly" seal.
