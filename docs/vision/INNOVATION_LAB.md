## Active Experiments

### Eco-Token Economy
- **Concept:** Digital tokens earned through conservation contributions
- **Status:** Designed, not implemented
- **Next step:** Prototype in single destination
- **Risk:** Economic complexity, regulatory uncertainty

### Species Identification AI
- **Concept:** Photo-based species identification using computer vision
- **Status:** Research phase
- **Next step:** Evaluate existing models (iNaturalist, Merlin)
- **Risk:** Accuracy requirements, training data availability

### Real-time Health Dashboard
- **Concept:** Live destination health visualization
- **Status:** Designed in operations capability
- **Next step:** Prototype with mock data
- **Risk:** Data availability, metric definition

## Prototype Ideas

### AR Trail Guide
- **Concept:** Augmented reality overlays on hiking trails
- **Description:** Point phone at landscape, see species names, historical info, trail conditions
- **Complexity:** High (AR framework, real-time data)
- **Dependencies:** Species database, GPS, Mapping
- **Status:** Concept only

### Voice-Guided Exploration
- **Concept:** Audio guides that respond to location and context
- **Description:** AI narrator that tells stories as you walk through a destination
- **Complexity:** Medium (TTS, location, content)
- **Dependencies:** Identity capability, Location services
- **Status:** Concept only

### Predictive Campaign Engine
- **Concept:** AI that creates and launches campaigns automatically
- **Description:** System detects declining visitor numbers, creates campaign, launches it
- **Complexity:** High (AI, campaign management, economics)
- **Dependencies:** Intelligence, Operations, Economy
- **Status:** Concept only

### Cross-Destination Species Tracking
- **Concept:** Track species migration across multiple destinations
- **Description:** Visitor observations in Destination A connect to observations in Destination B
- **Complexity:** Very High (multi-destination, AI matching)
- **Dependencies:** Community, Intelligence, Multi-tenant
- **Status:** Concept only

### Community Story Marketplace
- **Concept:** Visitors pay for access to premium community stories
- **Description:** Local storytellers monetize their knowledge through the platform
- **Complexity:** Medium (economy, content management)
- **Dependencies:** Identity, Economy, Billing
- **Status:** Concept only

### Digital Heritage Twins
- **Concept:** Virtual replicas of heritage sites
- **Description:** 3D scans of historical buildings, monuments, cultural sites
- **Complexity:** Very High (3D scanning, rendering, storage)
- **Dependencies:** Identity, PWA Engine
- **Status:** Concept only

## Future Experiments

### Wildlife Camera Networks
- **Concept:** Community-maintained camera traps connected to platform
- **Description:** Visitors deploy and maintain cameras, footage analyzed by AI
- **Complexity:** High (hardware, AI, community management)
- **Dependencies:** Ecology, Community, AI

### Underwater Species Mapping
- **Concept:** Diver-submitted underwater species observations
- **Description:** SCUBA and snorkelers contribute to marine biodiversity maps
- **Complexity:** High (underwater GPS, species ID, data validation)
- **Dependencies:** Marine monitoring, Community

### Acoustic Ecology
- **Concept:** Sound-based ecosystem monitoring
- **Description:** Microphones record ambient sounds, AI identifies species by call
- **Complexity:** Very High (audio processing, AI, hardware)
- **Dependencies:** Ecology, AI, IoT

### Carbon Footprint Tracking
- **Concept:** Measure visitor carbon footprint per destination
- **Description:** Calculate emissions from travel, accommodation, activities
- **Complexity:** Medium (data sources, calculation models)
- **Dependencies:** Visitor tracking, External data

### Cooperative Revenue Model
- **Concept:** Revenue sharing between platform, destinations, and communities
- **Description:** Every transaction benefits the ecosystem, not just the platform
- **Complexity:** Very High (economics, governance, legal)
- **Dependencies:** Economy, Governance, Billing

## Experiments to Avoid

### Social Features Without Purpose
- **Why avoid:** Social features that don't contribute to ecology or community are distractions
- **Better:** Social features tied to conservation or cultural preservation

### Gamification Without Education
- **Why avoid:** Points without learning are addictive, not valuable
- **Better:** Every gamification element teaches something

### AI Without Human Oversight
- **Why avoid:** AI decisions about culture and ecology require human validation
- **Better:** AI assists humans, humans decide

### Scale Without Community
- **Why avoid:** Scaling without strong communities creates empty platforms
- **Better:** Grow communities first, scale infrastructure second
