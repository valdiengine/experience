## Ecological Monitoring

### Citizen Science Data Quality
- How reliable are tourist-generated ecological observations?
- What validation mechanisms ensure data quality at scale?
- How does observer bias affect species distribution data?
- Can AI filter reliable from unreliable observations?

### Species Identification Accuracy
- What accuracy can AI achieve for species identification from photos?
- How does image quality affect identification accuracy?
- Can crowdsourced identification rival expert identification?
- What's the minimum number of observations needed for reliable population estimates?

### Ecosystem Health Metrics
- What metrics best represent ecosystem health?
- How do you aggregate diverse indicators into a single score?
- Can tourism data predict ecological decline before it happens?
- How do seasonal variations affect health measurements?

### Climate Impact Measurement
- Can tourism data detect climate impact on species distribution?
- How do you separate climate effects from tourism effects?
- What's the minimum observation period needed for climate trend detection?
- Can citizen science complement professional climate monitoring?

## Tourism Intelligence

### Visitor Behavior Prediction
- Can visitor patterns be predicted with sufficient accuracy for destination management?
- What data sources are most predictive of visitor behavior?
- How do you balance prediction accuracy with privacy concerns?
- Can predictions be made without individual tracking?

### Destination Health Prediction
- Can destination health be predicted from current trends?
- What leading indicators signal destination decline?
- How do you model complex social-ecological systems?
- Can AI recommend preventive actions before problems manifest?

### Economic Impact Measurement
- How do you measure tourism's economic impact on local communities?
- Can you attribute economic changes to specific tourism initiatives?
- How do you measure indirect economic effects (supply chains, multiplier effects)?
- What metrics capture community economic wellbeing beyond revenue?
